When expectations become too much.

A Shape of Her
← Back

A Shape of Her is a project about what people expect from women in China, and how this affects the way women live. People can tell a girl what she should be like long before she knows what she wants. I follow a woman through different stages of life to see how these expectations change. I’m interested in what happens when she wants something different, and how she finds a way to choose for herself.

A girl may be praised for staying quiet. As she gets older, people may care more about how she looks than what she thinks. Decisions about work can become harder when her family expects her to get married and have children. Looking after others takes time, and her own plans may have to wait. Even later in life, she may still feel that what she wants comes second. These experiences vary across China. A woman’s options can depend on where she lives and how much money her family has. The way her family treats her matters too.
