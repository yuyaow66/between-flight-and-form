A shape of her
Visual notes / 2026

I collect questions that express social expectations of women in China. Interviews with women around me help me understand how those expectations enter everyday life, and how each woman makes choices of her own.

Listening to my mother ↗02 / 05

My mother’s responsibilities and the patience she wishes she had.

Case study / 01
The women in my life

My mother cares for older and younger family members while helping run the factory. She says she is happy with her life, although the responsibilities can feel like a lot. When I ask what she would do differently, she wishes she had raised me with more patience.

Listening to my grandmother ↗03 / 05

Looking back
on marriage.

Regretting a decision doesn’t mean she loves her family any less.

Case study / 02
What each generation has to say

My grandmother says she is happy now and feels little pressure. Looking back, she regrets getting married and leaving the village she loved. She remembers the work after the move as exhausting. Her account makes room for both contentment and regret.

In the Mood

Film study / 03
A connection to everyday life

In the Mood for Love makes me think about what a woman can say while trying to be a good wife in other people’s eyes. I bring that question to my grandmother’s account of marriage.

When she speaks about regret, I want to understand what she means instead of reassuring her before I have listened.
